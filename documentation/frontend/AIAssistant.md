# Infinity AI Assistant

The Infinity AI Assistant is a state-of-the-art management companion that helps you troubleshoot, configure, and optimize your ARK servers using natural language.

## Capabilities

The assistant has deep access to the manager's internal state and knowledge base:
- **Troubleshooting**: Ask the AI to analyze your logs for crash reasons.
- **Config Help**: "How do I increase the taming speed for only small dinos?"
- **Mod Suggestions**: "Recommend some quality-of-life mods for a PvE server."
- **Automation Advice**: "Set up a daily restart schedule for me."

## Multi-Session Management

Just like modern AI platforms, Infinity AI supports multiple conversations:
- **New Chat**: Start a fresh context for a different problem.
- **Chat History**: Your previous conversations are saved locally, allowing you to refer back to past solutions.
- **Persistent Context**: The AI remembers which server you are currently managing and tailors its responses accordingly.

## Safety & Privacy

- **Local First**: The AI does not send your passwords or sensitive server data to external servers.
- **Sandboxed Execution**: Any suggestions provided by the AI must be manually approved or applied by the user.

---
*Tip: You can use the "Ask AI" button directly from the Config Editor or Log Viewer to get contextual help for those specific pages.*
