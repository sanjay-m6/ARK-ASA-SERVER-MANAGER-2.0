# 📜 Tribe Log Viewer

The Tribe Log Viewer is a specialized forensic tool designed to parse and visualize the rich historical data generated by your ARK: Survival Ascended tribes. It transforms raw log files into a readable, searchable, and beautiful chronological timeline of your server's narrative.

## 📝 Page Overview
- **Route**: `/tools/tribelog`
- **Purpose**: Parsing in-game tribe history, auditing player events, tracking taming/combat milestones, and administrative investigation.
- **Aesthetic**: Historical "Violet & Purple" interface featuring chronological timelines, color-coded event cards, and high-impact metric counters.

## 🚀 Key Modules

### 1. Chronological Timeline Architecture (⏳)
Experience your server's history as a narrative:
- **Day-Based Grouping**: Events are automatically organized by their in-game "Day" timestamp, providing a structured look at the server's progression over time.
- **Event Forensics**: Raw log strings are parsed into semantic events. The manager identifies exactly what happened—whether a creature was tamed, a structure was demolished, or a player was killed.
- **Visual Timeline Flow**: High-fidelity event cards feature a vertical timeline path with color-coded "Status Dots," allowing you to trace the server's activity at a glance.

### 2. High-Impact Event Metrics (📊)
Instant visibility into server milestones:
- **Taming Totals**: Track the total number of creatures tamed across the server or by specific tribes.
- **Combat Auditing**: See the volume of "Killed" events, including both creature deaths and player-to-player fatalities.
- **Structural Integrity**: Monitor destruction volume, tracking how many structures have been demolished or destroyed in raids.
- **Member Dynamics**: Audit tribe growth and shrinkage through "Member Added" and "Member Left" event tracking.

### 3. Advanced Filtering & Search (🔍)
Surgically discover critical information:
- **Event-Type Badges**: Use interactive filters to isolate specific categories of events. Want to see only the tames from the last 100 days? One click on the "Tamed" badge isolates exactly what you need.
- **Full-Text Search Engine**: Rapidly find events involving specific players, tribes, or dinosaur species. The search engine scans through thousands of entries in real-time.
- **Categorical Color-Coding**: A consistent visual language makes scanning easy:
    - **Red**: Fatalities and combat.
    - **Pink**: Successful tames and new additions.
    - **Amber**: Structure destruction and decay.
    - **Cyan**: Tribal growth and recruitment.

### 4. Administrative Utility Suite (🛠️)
Professional tools for server oversight:
- **Multi-Server Switching**: Quickly jump between different servers in your cluster to compare activity levels and historical data.
- **Real-Time Data Refresh**: Pull the latest logs directly from the server's filesystem with a single click, ensuring your view is always up to date.
- **Responsive Data Grid**: The interface handles thousands of log entries with high-performance virtualization, ensuring a smooth experience even on high-population servers.

## 🛠️ Interface Controls
- **Select Server [Dropdown]**: Choose which server's tribe logs to audit.
- **Refresh [RefreshCw]**: Manually trigger a fresh parse of the log files.
- **Search [Input]**: Filter logs by keyword, name, or event details.
- **Event Filters [Buttons]**: Toggle visibility of specific event types (Deaths, Tames, Destruction).
- **Timeline Explorer [Scroll]**: Navigate through the chronological history of the server.

## 🎨 Design Notes
- **Archival Aesthetic**: Uses vibrant violet and purple accents to differentiate "Historical Data" from active server management tools.
- **Premium Glassmorphism**: Features semi-transparent panels and subtle hover effects that feel native to the "Liquid Glass" design system.
- **Semantic Iconography**: Extensive use of specialized icons (Skull, Heart, Hammer) provides instant visual context to technical data.
